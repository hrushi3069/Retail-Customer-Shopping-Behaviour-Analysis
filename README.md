# Retail Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis
## Dashboard Preview

## Project Summary

This project analyses retail customer shopping behaviour to help businesses improve sales performance, customer engagement, customer loyalty, and marketing effectiveness.

## Business Problem Statement

A retail company wants to better understand its customers' purchasing patterns and behaviours. By analysing customer demographics, product preferences, discounts, shipping methods, reviews, and purchase history, the company can identify opportunities to increase revenue and strengthen customer relationships.

## Main Business Question

How can the company use customer shopping data to identify purchasing trends, improve customer engagement, and optimise marketing and product strategies?

## SQL Analysis

The following business questions were explored using SQL:

1. **Revenue Analysis** – Compared the total revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. **Discount & Spending Analysis** – Identified customers who used discounts and made purchases equal to or above the overall average purchase amount.
3. **Product Rating Analysis** – Ranked the top 5 products based on their average review ratings.
4. **Shipping Analysis** – Compared the average purchase amounts for Standard and Express shipping.
5. **Discount Dependency Analysis** – Identified the top 5 products with the highest percentage of purchases made using discounts.
6. **Customer Segmentation** – Classified customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments based on their previous purchases.
7. **Product Popularity Analysis** – Identified the top 3 most purchased products within each product category.
8. **Repeat Buyer Analysis** – Compared subscription status among customers with more than 5 previous purchases.
9. **Age Group Revenue Analysis** – Compared total revenue across different customer age groups.
